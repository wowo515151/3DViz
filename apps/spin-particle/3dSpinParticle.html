MODEL SCOPE

Particle properties and baseline couplings use CODATA 2022 values. The solver scales SI values to Å, 10⁶ m/s, and 0.1 fs for numerical stability. Hydrogenic preset orbits are classical initial conditions, not quantum states. Softening is an artificial length scale; radiation, retardation, induced electric fields, and relativistic effects are omitted.
